🚀 Key Features to Prioritize
Phase 1: Foundation (MVP)
Property Portfolio: Add/manage properties with photos, amenities, location details
Tenant Profiles: Basic info, contact details, documents storage
Lease Management: Digital lease agreements, rent amounts, terms
Rent Collection: Monthly rent tracking, payment status, overdue alerts
Basic Financials: Income/expense tracking, simple reports
Phase 2: Enhanced Management
Maintenance Module: Work orders, contractor database, cost tracking
Document Management: Secure storage for leases, IDs, receipts, legal documents
Communication Hub: In-app messaging, automated reminders, notifications
Tenant Screening: Background verification workflows
Financial Reports: Profit/loss statements, occupancy rates, ROI calculations
Phase 3: Advanced Features
Multi-unit Complexes: Buildings with multiple units, common area management
Automated Workflows: Rent escalation, lease renewals, compliance reminders
Integration Layer: Payment gateways, SMS/email services, property tax APIs
Mobile App: Dedicated apps for landlords and tenants
Analytics Dashboard: Market insights, property performance metrics
🤔 Questions to Shape Our Development
Target Users: Are you focusing on individual landlords, property managers, or both? What's the primary user segment?

Property Scale: Will this handle single properties or portfolio management (multiple properties)?

Payment Integration: How important is automated rent collection vs manual tracking?

Legal Compliance: Do you want to include Indian state-specific legal templates and compliance tracking?

Offline Capabilities: Should it work offline for property visits and inspections?

Integration Priority: Which third-party services matter most? (Payment processors, SMS gateways, document signing, etc.)

Monetization: Freemium model, subscription tiers, or transaction-based fees?
